# Research Report Benchmark

## Benchmark Description

This benchmark evaluates the ability of the AstralNexus system to handle cross-domain research tasks that involve:

1. Reading and understanding various types of materials (PDF, web pages, local documents)
2. Extracting facts and evidence from sources
3. Identifying conflicts and inconsistencies in information
4. Forming conclusions based on evidence
5. Generating comprehensive decision reports

## Test Cases

### Test Case 1: Technology Feasibility Analysis

**Scenario**: Analyze the feasibility of implementing a new technology solution for a business problem. The system should:

1. Research the technology and related approaches
2. Identify pros and cons of different options
3. Analyze implementation challenges and requirements
4. Evaluate costs and benefits
5. Generate a feasibility report with recommendations

**Success Criteria**:
- Comprehensive research covering multiple sources
- Clear identification of advantages and disadvantages
- Realistic assessment of implementation challenges
- Well-reasoned recommendations based on evidence
- Proper citation of sources

### Test Case 2: Market Analysis and Competitive Landscape

**Scenario**: Conduct a market analysis for a new product idea. The system should:

1. Research the target market and customer segments
2. Analyze competitors and their offerings
3. Identify market trends and opportunities
4. Evaluate potential risks and challenges
5. Generate a market analysis report with strategic recommendations

**Success Criteria**:
- Thorough market research with relevant data
- Detailed competitor analysis
- Identification of key trends and opportunities
- Balanced assessment of risks and challenges
- Actionable strategic recommendations

## Evaluation Metrics

1. **Research Completeness**: Coverage of relevant sources and topics
2. **Evidence Quality**: Accuracy and relevance of extracted facts
3. **Conflict Resolution**: Ability to identify and resolve conflicting information
4. **Logical Consistency**: Coherence and logical flow of arguments
5. **Report Quality**: Clarity, structure, and completeness of the final report
6. **Source Citation**: Proper attribution and referencing of sources
7. **Decision Support**: Usefulness of recommendations for decision-making

## Scoring

Each test case is scored on a scale of 1-10 for each metric:

- 1-3: Poor
- 4-6: Fair
- 7-8: Good
- 9-10: Excellent

Final score is the weighted average of all metrics.
